I am an assistant professor in the department of computer science at <a href="https://compsci.colostate.edu/">Colorado State University</a>. My research lies in **probability theory**, **non-commutative analysis** and **mathematical physics**.

Previously, I was a post-doctoral scholar at <a href="https://tcs.soe.ucsc.edu/trg">UC Santa Cruz</a> hosted by Prof. <a href="https://people.ucsc.edu/~akolla/">Alexandra Kolla</a>. I got my Ph.D. in computer science from <a href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.seas.harvard.edu%2Fcomputer-science&sa=D&sntz=1&usg=AFQjCNGjg26QHPZ0TDV_KVdv3VHJ0ZsKYg">Harvard university</a> advised by Prof. <a href="https://www.google.com/url?q=https%3A%2F%2Fsites.google.com%2Fview%2Ftuftsqi&sa=D&sntz=1&usg=AFQjCNHcsMTHG5jtH46FfNZ8OHvDqzM97w">Peter Love</a> and Prof. <a href="https://www.boazbarak.org/">Boaz Barak</a>. During my Ph.D., I visited Prof. <a href="https://lucatrevisan.github.io/">Luca Trevisan</a> at <a href="https://www.unibocconi.eu/wps/wcm/connect/Bocconi/SitoPubblico_EN/Navigation+Tree/Home/faculty+and+research/departments/Decision+Sciences/">Bocconi university</a>. 

I am in favor of <a href="https://www.slow-science.com/">slow science</a>, <a href="https://mathandai.org/">human understanding</a>, and enjoy **climbing** & **time-trialling** on my <a href="https://www.strava.com/athletes/190266479">bike</a>.
